Privacy Policy

Last updated:

AstroReveal (“we”, “us”) is run by . This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use astroreveal.in, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. We follow the principles of India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

1. What we collect

DataWhy we need it
Your email address, and either a hashed password or your Google account IDTo create and secure your account and sign you in. We never store your password in readable form.
Birth details you enter for yourself or others: name, date, time and place of birth (and the coordinates of that place), and how the person is related to youTo calculate a Kundli and generate readings and answers.
Palm photos you uploadTo generate a palm reading. The photo itself is not saved; only the text of the reading is stored.
Questions you ask in chat and the answersTo provide the chat feature and show your history.
Payment records: order and payment IDs, amount, date, statusTo deliver what you bought, resolve payment problems and meet accounting rules. Card, UPI and bank details are entered on Razorpay’s pages and are never seen or stored by us.
Basic technical data such as IP address and timestamps in server logsSecurity, abuse prevention and fixing errors.

We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising. We store a sign-in token in your browser’s local storage to keep you logged in; we do not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google’s sign-in script runs on our page under Google’s own policies.

2. Other people’s data

You may add family members or friends. By ticking the consent box you confirm that you have that person’s permission, or that you are their parent or legal guardian. Our service is intended for adults (18+). If you add a child’s details, you are doing so as their parent or guardian. If you believe someone added your details without permission, contact us and we will delete them.

3. Who handles your data for us

  • Google (Gemini AI): generates Kundli interpretations, palm readings and chat answers. We send the calculated chart (planets, houses and similar) without the person’s name or place of birth, your question, and palm photos when you request a palm reading. Google’s handling is governed by Google’s own terms.
  • Razorpay: payment processing.
  • Railway: hosting of our website, servers and database. Our hosting provider may store data on servers outside India.
  • Google Sign-In: only if you choose to sign in with Google.

We may also disclose data if the law or a court requires it.

4. How long we keep it

  • Your account data, saved people, Kundlis, readings and chats are kept until you delete them: you can delete a person or your whole account inside the app, and it is removed immediately.
  • If an account has been inactive for 24 months, we may delete it automatically.
  • Payment records (amount, date, order ID; no name or email) are kept for as long as accounting and tax rules require. Copies of payment notifications that contain your contact details are deleted with your account.
  • Server logs are kept only for a limited period needed for security.

5. Your rights

You can: see your data in the app; correct it (Edit on a saved person); erase it (Delete a person, or Delete account); withdraw consent by deleting your account; and complain to us about how your data is handled. To exercise a right you cannot do in the app, email .

6. Security

Connections use HTTPS, passwords are stored hashed, and access to our systems is restricted. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a strong password and tell us at once if you suspect misuse.

8. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.
