Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated:

AstroReveal sells digital services that are delivered instantly in your account, and there are no subscriptions or auto-renewals, so there is nothing to cancel later. Here is when we refund.

We refund in full when

  • You were charged but your credits were not added, and we cannot fix it within 24 hours.
  • You were charged twice for the same purchase.
  • You bought credits and have not used any of them, and you ask within 24 hours of the purchase.
  • A reading failed because of a technical error on our side and the credit was not restored.

We do not refund

  • Credits that have already been used to generate a Kundli, palm reading or chat answer, because the service has been delivered.
  • Credits that have expired (they are valid for 30 days).
  • Dissatisfaction with what a reading says. Readings are for reflection and entertainment and are not guaranteed to match your expectations.
  • Wrong birth details entered by you after the free correction window has ended.

How to ask for a refund

Email from the address on your account, with your Razorpay payment or order ID and a short description. We reply within 2 business days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5–7 business days depending on your bank or card issuer.
